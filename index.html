Profile

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Biomedical Imaging Group at EPFL and the CIBM Center for Biomedical Imaging, working with Prof. Michaël Unser. My research develops mathematical and computational methods for acquiring and reconstructing signals beyond conventional sensing limits.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science, advised by Prof. Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula. My work connects sampling theory, inverse problems, optimization, and computational imaging.
