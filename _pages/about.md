I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Biomedical Imaging Group at EPFL](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/) and the [CIBM Center for Biomedical Imaging](https://cibm.ch), working with [Prof. Michaël Unser](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/unser/). My research develops mathematical and computational methods for acquiring and reconstructing signals beyond conventional sensing limits.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Electrical Engineering at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in), advised by [Prof. Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula](https://spectrum.ee.iisc.ac.in/). My work connects sampling theory, inverse problems, optimization, and computational imaging.
